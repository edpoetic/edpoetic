# Customer Support Playbook
**DEMONSTRATION SAMPLE**

## Response framework
1. Acknowledge the customer's concern.
2. Confirm the relevant order/account details securely.
3. Explain the next step and expected response window.
4. Escalate issues outside the agent's authority.
5. Log the interaction and close the loop.

## Sample response
“Thank you for letting us know. I'm checking the order details now and will update you as soon as I have confirmation. For your security, please do not send payment card details or passwords in this chat.”

## Escalation matrix
| Issue | First action | Escalate to |
|---|---|---|
| Late delivery | Check tracking | Fulfilment |
| Payment discrepancy | Verify transaction reference | Finance |
| Damaged item | Request approved evidence | Returns team |
| Account access | Follow identity verification | Technical support |

*Illustrative scripts; actual policies must be supplied by the client.*
