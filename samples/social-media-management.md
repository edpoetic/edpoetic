# Social Media Management Sample
**DEMONSTRATION SAMPLE · Fictional lifestyle brand**

## Content pillars
- Education: practical planning and organisation tips.
- Product: features, use cases, and product FAQs.
- Community: prompts, polls, and customer questions.
- Brand story: values, process, and behind-the-scenes content.

## One-week calendar
| Day | Format | Topic | Objective |
|---|---|---|---|
| Monday | Carousel | Plan a realistic week | Saves |
| Tuesday | Story | This-or-that planning poll | Replies |
| Wednesday | Reel | 15-second desk reset | Reach |
| Friday | Static | Weekly reflection prompt | Engagement |

## Publishing checklist
- Copy proofread
- Visual checked on mobile
- CTA included
- Links tested
- Scheduled time confirmed
- Comments and inbox monitoring assigned

*No engagement or reach metrics are claimed.*
