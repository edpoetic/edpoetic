# English–Indonesian Translation Sample
**DEMONSTRATION SAMPLE**

## Source (English)
Please review the attached order summary and confirm whether the delivery address is correct. If any details need to be changed, reply before the order is processed.

## Translation (Bahasa Indonesia)
Mohon periksa ringkasan pesanan yang terlampir dan konfirmasikan apakah alamat pengiriman sudah benar. Jika ada detail yang perlu diubah, silakan balas sebelum pesanan diproses.

## Translation notes
- “Order summary” translated as “ringkasan pesanan” for a clear customer-facing tone.
- “Delivery address” translated as “alamat pengiriman”.
- The polite imperative is retained without making the Indonesian sentence overly formal.
