# Virtual Assistant Workflow
**DEMONSTRATION SAMPLE**

## Daily inbox triage
| Priority | Example request | Action | Target |
|---|---|---|---|
| High | Customer cannot access order | Acknowledge, verify details, escalate | Same business day |
| Medium | Meeting time confirmation | Check calendar, confirm attendees | Within 1 business day |
| Low | General information request | Use approved FAQ, log response | Within 2 business days |

## Daily routine
- Start: review calendar, inbox, and overdue tasks.
- Midday: process routine requests and update trackers.
- End of day: send concise status summary and flag blockers.
